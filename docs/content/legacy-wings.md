Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations Delhi-NCR:Spiritual Wing
Three Wings: Spiritual Wing, Service Wing, Education Wing
The Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations, Delhi-NCR is a spiritual organisation established and
founded by Bhagwan Sri Sathya Sai Baba. A key highlight pertains to the word ‘Seva’ as the
underlying guiding light that sets the context and foundation of the Organisation’s true
mission. Each of the three wings function seamlessly to put into practice the ‘Seva’ aspect.
Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations Delhi-NCR: Spiritual Wing.
Spirituality is an inherent quality in every person but in the hectic, fast-paced rush of life,
it is either dormant or not awakened. The true power of spirituality is such that it can
transform an animal into man and man into divine. Bhagawan’s teachings often refer to how
Valmiki transformed his dacoit existence into a divine journey by chanting the holy name of
‘Rama’ and how the young Prahlad overcame his fear by constantly chanting the name of Lord
Vishnu. These divine teachings highlight the importance of nurturing spiritual practices and
weaving it into our daily lives, transmuting every moment into something more sublime for
ourselves.
Thus, the Spiritual Wing undertakes activities that strengthen our deepest self through the
contemplation of the divine by different means - Veda chanting, Bhajans, Jyoti Meditation, - all
of which deepen our inner yearning to reconnect with the Divine and experience peace and love
within.
“The Sai Organisations were set up to make people realise their true divine nature. What
is the primary purpose of the Sai Organisations?”
It is to make the people realise
that they are sparks of the Divine and not embodiments of the physical products of Nature. The
inherent divinity of man has been clearly proclaimed in the Geetha.”.
-Sri Sathya Sai Speaks, Vol 28, 1995

Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations Delhi-NCR: Education Wing
The focus of education in the Sai system is in the holistic development of the human personality.
This education is aimed at bringing out the latent divinity in man by teaching human values like
truth, righteousness, peace, love, and non-violence. Such education is not confined to any
particular age, but is to be given throughout life, so that man can become what he truly is - a
divine being.

## wings_service
Service Wing
Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations
Delhi-NCR
Sri Sathya Sai Seva Organisations Delhi-NCR: Service Wing
Neither by penance nor by taking baths in sacred waters; neither by the study of scriptures nor
through meditation can one cross this ocean of worldly life. This ocean of worldly life can only
be crossed by rendering service to the good. - Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba (Divine Discourse,
19th November 1987)
“Manava Seva, Madhava Seva”
The divine life, message and teachings of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba has always been
‘Love All, Serve All’ and ‘Help Ever, Hurt Never’.
Bhagawan says, “Service done
with reverence and selflessness leads man to
‘Sakshatkara’
- Realisation of the
Divine.” (Sanathana Sarathi, 1986)
The emphasis on serving humanity with love forms the crux of Bhagawan’s divine mission and
teachings. In Bhagawan’s divine words, “Hands that serve are holier than lips that pray.”
“Wherever you are, whatever the time, when you meet situations that require help, render it to
the best of your capacity.” - Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba (Divine Discourse, 4th March 1960)
“Know that selfless service is a better form of spiritual discipline than even meditation. How
can God appreciate the meditation you do when adjacent to you, you have someone in agony, whom
you do not treat kindly, for whom you do not make all efforts to help?” (Sri Sathya Sai Speaks,
February 1, 1970)
Service Wing: Activities
● Narayana Seva
● Medical Camps
● Vocational Training
● Visit to Hospitals
● Visit to Old Age Homes
● Visit to Orphanages
● Grama Seva
