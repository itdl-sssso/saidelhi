WhatsApp → event → impact 
How a planned activity moves from a coordinator’s WhatsApp message to the public site. Only registered coordinators can plan. 
 Plan Coordinator messages the bot: type, date, venue, volunteers needed WhatsApp bot 
 Draft Bot reads it into an event draft and asks the coordinator to confirm Portal → Events (planned) 
 District approval Approve / Reject / Ask on WhatsApp. Reminder on day 2; escalates to state after 3 days Portal → Approvals (fallback) 
 Published Appears on Calendar, Event, Centre, District and Home’s upcoming strip Public site 
 Form links sent Report and desk links to the coordinator on WhatsApp; QR for the camp desk Portal → Form links 
 Reported Totals via form, Excel upload, WhatsApp or a scan. Non-form data goes to the review inbox Portal → Review inbox 
 Approved District approves; numbers appear on Event, Centre, District and Impact Public site 
 Report link · /f/<token> For the coordinator. WhatsApp OTP; only phones listed for the event. Event day to +14 days; reopens for surgery follow-up. 
 Desk link · /f/<token> One per registration desk, named. Camp day only. Offline drafts on the phone from the pilot. 
 Public link · /f/<token> Join an event as a volunteer. Rate-limited, bot check, consent. Counts only, no profiles.
